Out 15 October 2026

First study: the pushback test

We asked 8 widely used AI models hundreds of questions that have a correct answer. Then we pushed back, once and then five times in a row, and recorded when they gave up a correct answer.

Two pre-registered studies. Full results, method, prompts and data will be published here on 15 October.
